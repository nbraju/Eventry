FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about finding event vendors and planning your budget on Eventry AI Cloud.

What is Eventry AI Cloud?

Eventry AI Cloud is a free event vendor marketplace and AI-powered budgeting platform. It helps you discover verified vendors — tent houses, venues, caterers, decorators, AV/lighting providers and entertainers — near you, and plan a category-wise event budget with Plan vs Actual tracking before you book anything.

Which event vendor categories can I find on Eventry?

Seven categories: Tent House, Venue (banquet halls, function halls, resorts), Catering, Decorations, AV/Lighting (sound, DJ, stage lighting), Entertainer (anchors, dancers, bands) and Others (photography, transport and more).

Is there a booking commission or fee?

No. Listing your business and browsing vendors is free, with zero booking commission. Enquiries connect you directly with the vendor over WhatsApp or a phone call.

How does the event budget planner work?

Set your total budget, event date, guest count and location. Eventry AI Cloud automatically splits your budget across all seven categories, lets you pick real vendors from the live directory, and tracks Plan vs Actual spend with a contingency reserve and optional sponsorship contribution.

How do I list my event business on Eventry AI Cloud?

Use the "List" tab to submit your business details, or paste any WhatsApp message, email or voice transcript into "AI Onboard" and the AI will auto-extract your business name, category, phone, area and services. Listings are reviewed and usually go live within 1 hour.

Which cities and areas does Eventry AI Cloud cover?

Eventry AI Cloud is an India-wide vendor directory with active listings growing across Hyderabad and Telangana. Use "Near Me" in Browse to filter vendors within 25 km of your current location, or search by city/area.

Is Eventry AI Cloud an app I need to install?

It's a Progressive Web App (PWA) — open it in any mobile or desktop browser and optionally "Add to Home Screen" for an app-like experience. No app store download required.
